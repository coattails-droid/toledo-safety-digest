WORTH KNOWING (TOLEDO)
- Health
  - Measles in northwest Ohio: Bowling Green exposure window still open; call before you go in (updated)
    - What happened: with measles cases reported across northwest Ohio, ProMedica's Dr. Brian Kaminski says anyone who thinks they were exposed should phone their doctor, urgent care or hospital first so staff can prepare and protect others. He said the region has not reached the outbreak threshold (three or more linked cases from one source).
    - Lucas County: the confirmed case was an Amtrak Train 41 traveler from Chicago who arrived at the Toledo station about 11:40 p.m. Sep 13; the health department's monitoring of close contacts ran through Oct 5, and no spread from that case has been announced.
    - Wood County (Bowling Green): an adult who caught measles while traveling visited these places; anyone there may have been exposed:
      - BGSU Overman and/or Physical Science buildings, Sep 23, 9 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.
      - Pagliai's Restaurant, Sep 23, 5 to 9 p.m.
      - Wood County Hospital Emergency Department, Sep 24 8:30 p.m. to Sep 25 1:30 a.m., and Sep 25 6:30 a.m. to 2:15 p.m.
      - Falcon Health Center, Sep 28, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.
    - Symptoms can start up to 21 days after exposure, so watch through about Oct 14 (Sep 23 exposures) to Oct 19 (Sep 28 exposure): fever, runny nose, cough, red watery eyes, then a rash that starts at the hairline and spreads down. People are contagious four days before and four days after the rash starts.
    - What to do: if you were at those places, fill out the Measles Close Contact Investigation Form at woodcountyhealth.org; if you have symptoms, call ahead before going in; check that you and your kids are up to date on MMR.
    - Sources: https://www.13abc.com/2026/10/06/think-youve-been-exposed-measles-doctors-say-call-before-seeking-care/
