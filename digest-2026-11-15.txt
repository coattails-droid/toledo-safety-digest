NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota Tundra camshaft recall (new; Nov 13)
  - About 62,000 model year 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 non-hybrid Tundras; one supplier lot (~300 camshafts) built incorrectly; camshaft could snap causing loss of motive power at higher speeds.
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; dealer inspects and replaces at no cost; owner letters by mid-January 2027; Toyota 1-800-331-4331.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-model-year-2022-2023-and-2025-2026-toyota-tundra-vehicles/
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag recall (ongoing; Nov 12)
  - About 314,000 Toyota and Lexus vehicles; degraded inflator propellant can rupture and send metal fragments. Model list pending NHTSA documents.
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; free replacement. Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/
- Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel generators (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - ~3,000 dark gray units; valve issue can cause fuel ejection/fire/burn hazard. Stop use and contact Cummins.
