- NEW: Once Upon a Farm "Tractor Wheels" Organic Toddler Soft-Baked Bars - possible fatal choking hazard (FDA advisory Oct 30)
  - All flavors, sizes, lot codes and dates. FDA has several reports of toddlers (1.5-2.5 years) whose airway was completely blocked; the chewed bar forms a dry, sticky mass that back blows and the Heimlich may not clear.
