Toledo Safety Digest — Wednesday, September 30, 2026

Nothing requires shelter-in-place or a citywide water order today. Check your fridge for recalled Graziers raw-milk cheese (E. coli), and if you are near Cherry & Central, watch for lingering gasoline odors while repairs finish.

NEEDS ATTENTION

1) Graziers raw-milk cheese — E. coli O26:H11 (Class I)
All Sierra Nevada Cheese Company Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheeses (medium/sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, Monterey jack; retail and foodservice). Multistate outbreak: 13 ill, 8 hospitalized, 3 with HUS; Michigan among the states. Nationwide retail/online. Do not eat, sell, or serve; discard or return; sanitize surfaces that touched the cheese.
https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-e-coli-o26h11-raw-milk-cheese-september-2026
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/sierra-nevada-cheese-company-recalls-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-because-possible-health-risk

2) Cherry Oil / Old West End gasoline release — repairs still underway
Gasoline entered sewers after a vehicle hit a pump at Cherry St & Central Ave (Cherry Oil / Sunoco). Leak isolated from the sewer Sept 24; permanent underground repairs underway as of Sept 29; station closed; air monitoring continues. If you smell gasoline indoors nearby: ventilate, wet cloths / fill P-traps, cover drains; report to Engage Toledo at 419-936-2020.
https://www.13abc.com/2026/09/29/repairs-underway-toledo-gas-station-after-gas-leak-driver-still-missing/
https://toledo.oh.gov/news/2026/09/22/city-investigating-gasoline-odor-reports

3) Berlin Seeds alfalfa sprouting seed — STEC / Salmonella (Ohio)
About 950 lbs Berlin Seeds Alfalfa Sprouting Seed, lot SAL2-24JB (5-lb/50-lb; smaller bags may lack lot). Sold Feb 13–Aug 23, 2026 from Millersburg, OH and online to 44 states including OH and MI. Stop using; discard seed and any sprouts; sanitize equipment.
