- NEW: DREO space heaters, model DR-HSH015 - can overheat and ignite (CPSC Oct 29; heating season)
  - About 338,800 sold at Costco and online at Dreo.com, BestBuy.com, Kohls.com, Lowes.com, QVC.com, HomeDepot.com, B2BRenew.com and Costco.com, Sep 2025-Oct 2026 ($70-$110).   - What to do: stop using it now; unplug it, cut the cord, and send DREO a photo for a free replacement. 833-820-0887, spaceheater@realtimeresults.net, or www.recallrtr.com/spaceheater.
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/DREO-Recalls-DR-HSH015-Space-Heaters-Due-to-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards

- NEW: Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms that may not sound - CPSC stop-use warnings (Oct 29)
  - JuzhiAnn / Yfwsrecinoe / JAEHSKA combination alarms, model JZA-540COM, and GuardianSense / KLTseanife combination alarms, model YHW-542COM-R. CPSC says they can fail to alarm in smoke or fire.
  - What to do: check the model on the back of your alarms; if it matches, replace it with a working smoke alarm and CO detector right away, then dispose of it. No recall or refund was announced.
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls (Oct 29 warnings; full links in the repo file)

- NEW: NTI and HTP brand gas boilers (made by Ariston, formerly NTI Boilers) - fire, carbon monoxide and impact hazard (CPSC Oct 29)
  - 4,726 units. The condensate dish can crack during a delayed ignition, letting unburned gas build up; if it ignites, the front cover can blow off.
  - What to do: stop using it and call Ariston for a free repair by a certified technician (800-688-2575, https://ntiboilers.com/safety-recalls). If you must keep using it until the repair, make sure you have working smoke and CO alarms.
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls (Oct 29)
