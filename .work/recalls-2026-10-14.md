## NEW SINCE OCT 12–13

### Medications and supplements
- **NEW — Par Health Argatroban Injection 250 mg/2.5 mL (100 mg/mL) — particulate matter (hospital/user level)**
  - What happened / hazard: Voluntary nationwide recall of 22 lots for particulate matter (cellulose, polystyrene, PET, stopper material, possibly stainless steel). Administering product with particulates has reasonable probability of life-threatening effects: pulmonary emboli, vessel occlusions / tissue death / organ damage, vein-wall inflammation / clotting. No adverse events reported to company to date.
  - Product ID: NDC 42023-182-01 and 42023-182-89; 22 lots (see LONG LISTS). Direct thrombin inhibitor for HIT / PCI anticoagulation — **hospital injectable, not retail OTC**.
  - Where/when: Distributed nationwide to wholesalers and hospitals Dec 30, 2024 – May 5, 2026.
  - What to do: Wholesale/hospital accounts discontinue use and distribution; return via Inmar. Questions: Inmar 1-855-648-7909 (Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–5 p.m. ET) or rxrecalls@inmar.com. Medical/complaints: Par Health 1-800-828-9393. Report to FDA MedWatch.
  - Checklist category: Medications and supplements
  - Mark: **NEW** (announced Oct 13, 2026; not yet on FDA recalls landing table as of this morning’s fetch — company PR states conducted with FDA knowledge)

## TOYOTA / LEXUS (section 24) — recent for first day of section

### 1. MY2026 Toyota RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid — PHEV ECU / loss of motive power (recent; announced Oct 7)
- Models/years: Certain model year **2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid** (U.S.).
- Vehicle count: Approximately **31,000** U.S.
- Defect / risk: Fault in electronic control unit (ECU) for the plug-in hybrid system can shut the system down while driving → loss of motive power at higher speeds → increased crash risk. (USA Today NHTSA mirror: motor generator ECU may encounter an error causing PHEV system to reduce power or shut down at higher speeds.)
- Remedy / notification: Dealers update software of affected ECU at no cost. Owner notifications by **early December 2026**.
- NHTSA campaign: **26V659000** (USA Today NHTSA recall mirror titled “Motor Generator ECU Error May Cause Loss of Drive Power,” dated Oct 7, 2026). Toyota pressroom did not print the campaign number; check VIN for confirmation.
- VIN check: https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls or https://www.toyota.com/recall ; Toyota Brand Engagement Center 1-800-331-4331.
- Do-not-drive / park-outside: **No** (not stated in pressroom or NHTSA mirror).
- Mark: **NEW to digest** (section 24 first day; within ~2 weeks)
- Sources:
  - https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-my2026-toyota-rav4-plug-in-hybrid-electric-vehicles/
  - https://www.usatoday.com/recalls/automotive/NHTSA-26V659000/

### 2. MY2026 Toyota Grand Highlander / Lexus RX & TX — front steering knuckle may crack (recent; announced Sept 17; owner letters still pending)
- Models/years: 2026 Toyota Grand Highlander and Grand Highlander Hybrid; 2026 Lexus RX350, RX350 Hybrid, RX500 Hybrid, TX350, TX500 Hybrid.
- Vehicle count: **617**.
- Defect / risk: Manufacturing error — front steering knuckles may crack and break → loss of steering control → increased crash risk. (NHTSA campaign components: STEERING:LINKAGES:KNUCKLE:SPINDLE:ARM.)
- Remedy / notification: Dealers inspect and replace front steering knuckles as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters expected **November 2, 2026**. Toyota recall nos. **26TA19** / Lexus **26LA09**. Customer service 1-800-331-4331.
- NHTSA campaign: **26V598000**.
- VIN check: nhtsa.gov/recalls or toyota.com/recall (Lexus owners use same NHTSA VIN tool / Lexus recall channels as stated by manufacturer).
- Do-not-drive / park-outside: **No** (not flagged in NHTSA acknowledgment materials reviewed).
- Note: Announced Sept 17 (~4 weeks ago) — outside strict ~2-week window but still pre-notification and critical; include for first day of section 24 with date clear. Omit from “last 7 days” framing.
- Mark: **NEW to digest** (section 24 first day)
- Sources:
  - https://www.usatoday.com/recalls/automotive/NHTSA-26V598000/
  - NHTSA acknowledgment letter excerpt (autoevolution PDF attachment of Sept 21, 2026 letter): https://www.autoevolution.com/pdf/news_attachements/toyota-recalls-grand-highlander-lexus-tx-and-rx-for-steering-knuckles-that-may-fail-276080.pdf
