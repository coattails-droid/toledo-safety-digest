EMERGING TECH WATCH
- Geoengineering (updated)
  - Space sunshade study (reported Nov 3; confirmed research, not a deployment): Nature on Oct 28 featured an analysis by the U.S. nonprofit Planetary Sunshade Institute, funded with about 400,000 pounds from the U.K.'s ARIA research agency. It estimates that cutting sunlight about 1% (roughly 1 degree C of cooling) would take about 5,000 spinning reflectors about 40 km across near the Sun-Earth L1 point, and that construction could begin in 20-40 years. Cornell climate scientist Visioni told Nature deployment is unlikely before the century's end; it would not remove greenhouse gases. https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03415-2 ; https://www.dongascience.com/en/news/80604
  - Ongoing: Ohio HB 290 (ban on cloud seeding and solar modification) still in House committee; Carnegie's October "Planetary Power" paper on the geopolitics of climate intervention. No field trials or deployments found.
- 6G Cellular (ongoing)

Release 21's first freeze is March 2027, final freeze December 2028. FCC's Oct 29 proposal to auction 25 MHz of mid-band spectrum for direct-to-phone satellite service is open for comment. https://3gpp-explorer.com/articles/weekly-2026-w44/ ; https://www.3gpp.org/news-events/3gpp-news/rel21-timeline
