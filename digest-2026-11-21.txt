**NEEDS ATTENTION**
- Lucas County water main breaks (updated/ongoing; Nov 20)
  - 4 water main breaks in Washington Township prompting boil advisories; affected area includes all water customers north and east of the Summit Street and Shoreland Avenue roundabout, as well as most properties on the west side of Summit between Shoreland and the Michigan border (exception handful closest to Shoreland on west side). One repaired by late Nov 20; all expected complete early Nov 21. Advisory at least 72 hours pending lab tests. Attributed to aging pipe system/valve bolts.
  - What to do: Boil water for drinking/cooking at least 1 minute or use bottled water; flush taps; monitor Lucas County Sanitary Engineer or local news for lift notice.
  - Sources: https://www.toledoblade.com/local/environment/2026/11/20/boil-advisory-issued-for-eastern-washington-township/stories/20261120093 ; https://www.13abc.com/2026/11/20/water-main-breaks-prompt-boil-advisory-some-washington-twp-homes-businesses/
