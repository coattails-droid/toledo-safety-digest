NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota Tundra camshaft recall (ongoing; Nov 13)
  - About 62,000 model year 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 non-hybrid Tundras; one supplier lot (about 300 camshafts) built incorrectly; camshaft could snap, causing loss of motive power at higher speeds.
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; dealer inspects and replaces at no cost; owner letters by mid-January 2027; Toyota 1-800-331-4331.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-model-year-2022-2023-and-2025-2026-toyota-tundra-vehicles/
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag recall (ongoing; Nov 12)
  - About 314,000 vehicles; degraded inflator propellant can rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin; model list not yet published (NHTSA documents expected).
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; free inflator or airbag replacement. Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/
- Sharper Image tabletop and Infinity fire pits (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - About 265,000 units sold Jan 2023-Jun 2026 nationwide and online; "77" logo, lot code sticker, letters KS-CY or KS-FC; ignited fuel can escape (4 injuries incl. third/fourth-degree burns).
  - What to do: stop using; register at firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com for a full refund (mark "RECALLED - DO NOT USE", send 3 photos); dispose of fuel per local hazardous-waste rules. Source: https://www.productsafer.com/findings/allstar-recalls-about-265-000-sharper-image-tabletop-fire-pits-over-burn-and-fir
- Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel generators (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - About 3,000 units; valve installed incorrectly can build pressure and eject fuel or the fuel cap (11 reports): fire/burn hazard. Stop use and contact Cummins.
- Medline beds, rails, mattresses and accessories (ongoing; FDA Nov 13)
  - Potential fire hazard and entrapment risk; check the Medline notice. Source: https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts
- Huffy Buzz Cerana T2 e-trike E4004 (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - About 286 units; rear wheels can loosen or detach (12 reports, 3 injuries). Stop riding; free replacement axle assemblies; Huffy 855-430-6240.
- Boshengya rugs (new to digest; CPSC Nov 12): violate the mandatory carpet flammability standard (fire hazard); check CPSC notice.
