NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag inflator recall (NEW; Toyota Nov 12; about 314,000 U.S. vehicles)
  - Inflator propellant can degrade; rupture can send metal fragments into the cabin (injury or death). Model/year list and NHTSA campaign number were not in Toyota's release; check by VIN.
  - What to do: enter VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; free inflator or airbag-assembly replacement; Toyota 1-800-331-4331. https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/
- Sharper Image tabletop and Infinity fire pits - burns/fire (NEW; CPSC Nov 12; about 265,000)
  - Ignited liquid can escape or fuel containers can jet flame; 4 injuries incl. third/fourth-degree burns. Sold at Walmart, Target, Home Depot, Lowe's, Kohl's, Marshalls, TJ Maxx, Walgreens, Amazon and others Jan 2023-Jun 2026 ($30-50). Identify: "77" logo, lot-code sticker on base (KS-CY or KS-FC, date YYYY-MM-DD).
  - What to do: stop using; register at www.firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com for a refund (write RECALLED - DO NOT USE, submit 3 photos); dispose of fuel as hazardous waste. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Allstar-Recalls-Sharper-Image-Tabletop-and-Infinity-Fire-Pits-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Burn-and-Fire-Hazards
- Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel portable generators - fire/burn (NEW; CPSC Nov 12; about 2,960)
  - Incorrect valves let tank pressure build; fuel can discharge or the cap eject (11 reports; one person splashed in the face). Dark gray, 4,500-5,000 W. Sold at Camping World, Northern Tool and Cummins.com May 2025-Aug 2026 ($1,640-1,930).
  - What to do: stop using; check model/part number at https://www.cummins.com/en-na/na/generators/portable-generators/safety-recalls/campaigns/7308 ; free repair at a Cummins service location. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Cummins-Recalls-Portable-Generators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards
- Kakgold 4WD stunt vehicle RC toy - button/lithium battery ingestion (NEW; CPSC Nov 12; about 294)
  - Remote's battery compartment is accessible to children; swallowed button cells can cause burns or death. Lime green/blue car, white hang tag "Do Not Twist the Shaft"; Amazon Jul-Sep 2026 ($34-50). No injuries reported.
  - What to do: take the remote away from children; write RECALLED on it, email a disposal photo to kakrecall@outlook.com for a refund. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Kakgold-Recalls-4WD-Remote-Controlled-Car-Toys-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Battery-Ingestion-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Toys
- FDA tainted-supplement warnings (NEW; FDA Nov 12)
  - Candy Power For Man honey dietary supplement (Eros Apothecary; undeclared tadalafil) and 10 Day Forecast Capsules 3200 mg (Game Changer Products; sildenafil and acetaminophen). Ohio distribution not stated. Can interact dangerously with nitrates/heart medicines. Stop using and ask a doctor. https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts
- Ten-year-old struck by pickup, Manhattan Ave and Mulberry St (ongoing; WTVG Nov 9-10): serious condition; TPD investigating; no newer report found. Drivers: slow near crosswalks.
- Furnace/CO safety (ongoing): nights near 29; have heating checked by a licensed pro and test CO alarms.
- Carried recalls (ongoing, see Nov 12 file for details): Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks (CPSC Nov 5, about 3.66M); Bose S1 Pro battery packs (about 634,759); Gourmia GTF7530 air-fryer ovens (175,951); Baby Brezza sterilizer dryers BRZ0083/0154/0156 (29,000); Family Farmstead raw cheddar (Listeria); Laub's mini hay cubes (botulism, animal feed); Fresh To You biscuit sandwich (Listeria); Brad's kale chips (thallium); Eugia vancomycin vials (glass particles). https://github.com/coattails-droid/toledo-safety-digest/blob/main/digest-2026-11-12.txt
