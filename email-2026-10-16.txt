NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota Land Cruiser 2024-2027 - fuel leak / fire risk (NEW; Toyota Oct 14)
  - Fuel tube connection near the tank can come apart and leak fuel until the hybrid system shuts the engine off; fire risk near an ignition source. About 106,000 U.S. vehicles.
  - Free clamp (or fuel tube replacement); letters by mid-December. No do-not-drive/park-outside instruction; if you smell fuel, don't drive it. NHTSA number not yet posted.
  - Check VIN: https://www.toyota.com/recall or https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls ; 1-800-331-4331.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-my2024-2027-land-cruiser-vehicles/

  - What to do: stop use, unplug; register at https://www.scotchbrand.com/3M/en_US/scotch-brand/tl1302Z-laminator-recall/ with photo of cut cord for refund check. 800-772-4337.
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
