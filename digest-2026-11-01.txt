NEEDS ATTENTION
- Once Upon a Farm "Tractor Wheels" Organic Toddler Soft-Baked Bars - possible fatal choking hazard (ongoing; FDA advisory Oct 30)
  - All flavors, sizes, lot codes and dates. FDA has reports of toddlers (1.5-2.5 years) whose airways were completely blocked; the chewed bar can form a dry, sticky mass that back blows and the Heimlich may not clear. Company recall and reformulation under way.

  - What to do: don't serve them; throw them away. If a child is choking and first aid fails, call 911. FDA 1-888-463-6332.
