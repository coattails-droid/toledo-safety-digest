NEEDS ATTENTION
- Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks - fall / head injury (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 3,662,000)
  - Models: Adjustable Fortress 2.0, Conquest 2.0, Dark Horse 2.0, Epic, King Kombo XT, Leveler, MegaLite, MegaLite+, Multi, Quantum, Vector. Rapid Locks black or orange on each leg. Exclude units with an orange rectangular "QC" sticker on the rail above a Rapid Lock. Sold Dec 2015-Oct 2026 at Lowe's, Costco, Amazon, Little Giant site and others, about $100-$1,000. Firm reports 25 retracting incidents, 19 injuries (3 with TBI/skull fracture/concussion/vision or hearing loss/cognitive impairment).
  - What to do: stop using until you get the repair kit (warning labels and instructions); 866-201-9069 or https://rapidlockcustomereducation.expertinquiry.com / https://www.littlegiantladders.com/pages/recalls . If a lock still fails after lubrication/evaluation, replacement or refund options apply. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Little-Giant-Recalls-Ladders-with-Rapid-Locks-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fall-Hazard
- Bose S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ lithium-ion battery packs - fire (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 634,759 U.S. plus about 35,400 Canada)
  - Packs can overheat, melt, smoke or catch fire (7 reports; 6 with property damage; no injuries reported). Sold with speakers and as stand-alone/replacement packs.
  - What to do: stop using the pack; remove it from the speaker (speaker may still run on AC power); register serial at http://www.bose.com/support/s1-battery-recall for a free replacement; dispose of the pack per local lithium-ion rules (not household trash). 800-278-1083. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
- X-VESOLJE nursing pillows (Kikea Store) - infant suffocation (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 19,596)

- Magnetopia magnetic building cubes/blocks - magnet ingestion (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 7,600)
  - Magnets can come loose if cubes break. Models MAT-030 through MAT-040, T009, T025, T076, T078 (styles Darkhouse, Farmday, PinkHouse, Seafight, Firefarm, Darkhero). Sold Magnetopia.com Apr 2025-Oct 2026, $20-$40.
  - What to do: stop using; prepaid return for refund at recall.magnetopia.com. Swallowed magnet: ER now; Poison Help 1-800-222-1222. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Magnetopia-Recalls-Magnetic-Building-Cubes-and-Blocks-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Magnet-Ingestion-Hazard
