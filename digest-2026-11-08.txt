- Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks - fall / head injury (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 3,662,000)
  - Models: Adjustable Fortress 2.0, Conquest 2.0, Dark Horse 2.0, Epic, King Kombo XT, Leveler, MegaLite, MegaLite+, Multi, Quantum, Vector. Rapid Locks black or orange on each leg.   - What to do: stop using until you get the repair kit (warning labels and instructions); 866-201-9069 or https://rapidlockcustomereducation.expertinquiry.com / https://www.littlegiantladders.com/pages/recalls . If a lock still fails after lubrication/evaluation, replacement or refund options apply. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Little-Giant-Recalls-Ladders-with-Rapid-Locks-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fall-Hazard
- Bose S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ lithium-ion battery packs - fire (ongoing; CPSC Nov 5; about 634,759 U.S. plus about 35,400 Canada)
  - Packs can overheat, melt, smoke or catch fire (7 reports; 6 with property damage; no injuries reported). Sold with speakers and as stand-alone/replacement packs.
  - What to do: stop using the pack; remove it from the speaker (speaker may still run on AC power); register serial at http://www.bose.com/support/s1-battery-recall for a free replacement; dispose of the pack per local lithium-ion rules (not household trash). 800-278-1083. https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
