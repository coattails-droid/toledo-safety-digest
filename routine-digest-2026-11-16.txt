NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota Tundra camshaft recall (ongoing; Nov 13)
  - ~62,000 model year 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 non-hybrid Tundras; one supplier lot (~300 camshafts) built incorrectly; camshaft could snap -> loss of motive power at higher speeds, crash risk.
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; dealer inspects/replaces free; owner letters by mid-January 2027; Toyota 1-800-331-4331.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-model-year-2022-2023-and-2025-2026-toyota-tundra-vehicles/
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag recall (ongoing; Nov 12)
  - ~314,000 vehicles; degraded inflator propellant can rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin (injury/death). Model list not yet published (NHTSA documents expected).
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; free inflator or airbag replacement. Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/
- Sharper Image tabletop and Infinity fire pits (ongoing; CPSC)
  - ~265,000 units sold Jan 2023-Jun 2026 (Home Depot, Lowe's, Target, Walmart, Kohl's, Walgreens, Amazon etc., $30-$50); "77" logo, lot code sticker, letters KS-CY or KS-FC; ignited fuel can escape (4 injuries incl. 3rd/4th-degree burns).
  - What to do: stop using; register at firepitrecall.expertinquiry.com (mark "RECALLED - DO NOT USE", send 3 photos) for a full refund; dispose of fuel per local hazardous-waste rules; Allstar 855-215-5032. Source: https://www.productsafer.com/findings/allstar-recalls-about-265-000-sharper-image-tabletop-fire-pits-over-burn-and-fir
- Costway convertible high chairs (new; CPSC Nov 16)
  - ~3,300 units sold at Target, Walmart and Costway.com May 2023-Jul 2026; no restraint system: child can slip through opening, be trapped or fall. No injuries reported.
  - What to do: stop using; contact Costway for free replacement (receipt or photo; prepaid return label). Source: https://abc7news.com/post/more-3300-high-chairs-costway-recalled-safety-hazard-consumer-product-commission-says-heres-what-know/20034926/
- Fresh & Ready Foods Biscuit & Sausage Gravy, 9.5 oz (new; Nov 14; Listeria)
  - Same firm as the Nov 8 Fresh to You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit recall (Listeria monocytogenes). Check the FDA notice for lots/dates; do not eat, discard or return. Source: https://itsrecalled.com/recalls/this-week/ (FDA notice via fda.gov recalls page)
- Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel generators (ongoing; CPSC)
  - ~3,000 units; incorrectly installed valve can eject fuel or fuel cap (11 reports): fire/burn hazard. Stop use; contact Cummins.
- Medline beds, rails, mattresses and accessories (ongoing; FDA Nov 13): potential fire hazard and entrapment risk; see FDA notice https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts
- Huffy Buzz Cerana T2 e-trike E4004 (ongoing; CPSC): ~286 units; rear wheels can loosen/detach (12 reports, 3 injuries). Stop riding; free replacement axle assemblies; Huffy 855-430-6240.
- OsteoDenx capsules (updated Nov 14; undeclared milk): lots 0524100 (exp May 2027) and 0625041 (exp Aug 2028), 30- and 60-capsule cartons; company added two Amazon UPCs (857294007259, 857294007242) to the FDA-listed 857294007228 and 857294007341. Milk-allergic people: do not use. Source: https://smartorusa.com/bioquad-osteodenx-milk-recall-amazon-update-november-2026-en/
