NEEDS ATTENTION
- ONTHEWEI baby bath seats - drowning risk (NEW; CPSC recall Oct 8)
  - What happened: the seats violate the mandatory infant bath-seat standard because they are unstable and can tip over in use, a drowning risk. No incidents reported.
  - Product: ONTHEWEI-branded baby bath seats in gray, pink, blue and mist green, with detachable restraint arms and suction cups underneath. Model No: MY002; Date of manufacturer: 2026/1/10 printed on a label on the back. About 2,140 units.
  - Where and when sold: Amazon.com in April 2026, $40 to $50.
  - What to do: stop using immediately; write "RECALLED" on the front with permanent marker, remove the back rest and arm restraints, cut the three suction cups, email a photo of the destroyed seat to onthewei@outlook.com for a full refund, then throw it away. Never leave a baby unattended in a bath, even with a seat.
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/ONTHEWEI-Recalls-Baby-Bath-Seats-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-to-Children-from-Drowning-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Infant-Bath-Seats
