  - New (EPA, Oct 25 noon): on Sat afternoon EPA's TAGA mobile lab detected an "extremely low" level of glycidyl methacrylate in a highly industrial area, below reporting and screening levels; an hour of follow-up sampling there and the neighborhood route found nothing more. EPA launched a public data viewer: click a monitoring spot on the map for summary tables (PM2.5, PM10, carbon monoxide, hydrogen sulfide, VOCs, oxygen).
