NEEDS ATTENTION
- Toyota Tundra camshaft recall (new; Nov 13)
  - About 62,000 model year 2022-2023 and 2025-2026 non-hybrid Tundras; one supplier lot (~300 camshafts) built incorrectly; a camshaft could snap and cause loss of motive power at higher speeds.
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; dealer inspects and replaces at no cost; owner letters by mid-January 2027; Toyota 1-800-331-4331.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-certain-model-year-2022-2023-and-2025-2026-toyota-tundra-vehicles/
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag recall (ongoing; Nov 12)
  - About 314,000 Toyota and Lexus vehicles; degraded inflator propellant can rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin. Model list not yet published (NHTSA documents expected).
  - What to do: check VIN at Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls; free inflator or airbag replacement. Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/
- Cummins Onan P5000iDF EFI dual-fuel generators (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - ~3,000 dark gray units; valve installed incorrectly can build pressure, push fuel out or eject fuel cap (11 reports, one person splashed in face): fire/burn hazard. Stop use and contact Cummins. Source: https://cedarnews.net/newstasks/1142763/product-recall-consumer-safety-roundup-november-13/
- Medline beds, rails, mattresses and accessories (new; FDA Nov 13)
  - Potential fire hazard and entrapment risk; check Medline notice and FDA page. Source: https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts
- Huffy Buzz Cerana T2 e-trike, model E4004 (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12)
  - ~286 units; rear wheels can loosen or detach (12 reports, 3 injuries). Stop riding; free replacement axle assemblies; Huffy 855-430-6240. Source: https://krissys-newsletter-9ff8b4.beehiiv.com/p/recalls-friday-november-13-2026
- Sharper Image tabletop fire pits, ~265,000 (ongoing; CPSC Nov 12; details in the Nov 13 file)
- Small Craft Advisory, western Lake Erie (new)
  - NWS Cleveland, issued Nov 14 3:18 a.m. until 10 a.m. Sunday Nov 15 (zone LEZ144). Northwest winds; small boats should stay in port.
