Toledo Safety Digest — Sunday, October 4, 2026

Nothing needs shelter-in-place or a citywide water order today. NEEDS ATTENTION

1) Great Value & Trader Joe’s frozen organic berries — E. coli O145:H28 (ongoing; expanded Oct 2)
Frutas y Hortalizas del Sur (Chile) expanded its frozen-berry recall. Check freezers for:
• Great Value Organic Triple Berry Blend 10 oz, UPC 7874211226 — lots 6 031 01 and 6 041 01 (best-by Jan 31 / Feb 10, 2028)
• Great Value Organic Blueberries 10 oz, UPC 7874211213 — lots 6 019 01, 6 040 01, and 6 163 01 (best-by Jan 19 / Feb 9 / Jun 12, 2028)
• Trader Joe’s Organic Mixed Berry Blend 12 oz, UPC 00977654 — lot 6 051 01 (best before Feb 20, 2028)
Shipped to Walmart in 30 states including Ohio and Michigan, and to Trader Joe’s in 26 states including Ohio and Michigan. Linked to an outbreak declared over (17 illnesses in FL/GA; 6 hospitalized; no deaths). Do not eat; discard or return. Don’t use recalled frozen berries in smoothies or baking.
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/frutas-y-hortalizas-del-sur-sa-expands-recalls-include-additional-lots-great-value-frozen-organic
https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-e-coli-o145h28-frozen-blueberries-july-2026

2) Bettergoods Authentic Italian Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine — Listeria (Walmart; all lots)
Frozen 22 oz yellow package, UPC 194346442706 — all lot codes. Do not eat; return to Walmart or discard. Highest risk for pregnant people, young children, older adults, immunocompromised.
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/gias-foods-inc-recalls-bettergoods-authentic-italian-lemon-alfredo-fettuccine-because-possible-0

3) Graziers raw-milk cheese — E. coli O26:H11 (Class I, ongoing)
All Sierra Nevada Cheese Company Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheeses (medium/sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, Monterey jack). Multistate; Michigan among states. Check fridge and freezer; discard or return; sanitize surfaces.
https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-e-coli-o26h11-raw-milk-cheese-september-2026
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/sierra-nevada-cheese-company-recalls-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-because-possible-health-risk

4) Cherry Oil / Old West End gasoline release — repairs still underway (city update Oct 2)
Gasoline entered sewers after a vehicle hit a pump at Cherry St & Central Ave. City Oct 2: contractor excavated the site, found the leak in the gasoline line and a break in the owner’s sewer lateral; the property sewer line is blocked to stop any discharge into the city system; station remains closed until repairs are done; Environmental Services continues air monitoring. If you smell gasoline indoors nearby: ventilate, wet cloths / fill P-traps, cover drains; report Engage Toledo 419-936-2020.
https://toledo.oh.gov/news/2026/09/22/city-investigating-gasoline-odor-reports
https://www.13abc.com/2026/09/29/repairs-underway-toledo-gas-station-after-gas-leak-driver-still-missing/

SKY
Tonight mostly clear (lows mid-40s) after a mostly sunny day — good for Saturn. Saturn is at opposition today (Oct 4) — brightest of 2026 (~mag 0.3), rises near sunset in the east and is up all night in Cetus; any small telescope shows the rings (tilted ~7.5°). Look east after sunset for a golden “star” brighter than nearby stars. Minor Draconid meteor peak around Oct 8–9 (early evening, usually modest rates; New Moon Oct 10 helps dark skies later in the week). NOAA SWPC 27-day outlook: Kp max ~4 around Oct 4–6; no Kp 6+ aurora watch for northern Ohio.
