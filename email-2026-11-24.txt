**NEEDS ATTENTION**
- Lucas County water main breaks (updated/ongoing; Nov 20)
  - 4 water main breaks in Washington Township prompting boil advisories; affected area includes all water customers north and east of the Summit Street and Shoreland Avenue roundabout, as well as most properties on the west side of Summit between Shoreland and the Michigan border (exception handful closest to Shoreland on west side).
