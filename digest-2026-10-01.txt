Toledo Safety Digest — Thursday, October 1, 2026

Nothing needs shelter-in-place or a citywide water order today. Check the fridge/freezer for recalled Graziers raw-milk cheese (E. coli) and Bettergoods Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine at Walmart (Listeria), and if you are near Cherry & Central watch for lingering gasoline odors while repairs finish.

NEEDS ATTENTION

1) Graziers raw-milk cheese — E. coli O26:H11 (Class I, ongoing)
All Sierra Nevada Cheese Company Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheeses (medium/sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, Monterey jack; retail and foodservice). Multistate outbreak: 13 ill, 8 hospitalized, 3 with HUS; Michigan among the states. Sold nationwide and online. Do not eat, sell, or serve; discard or return; sanitize surfaces that touched the cheese. Long shelf life — check fridge and freezer.
https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-e-coli-o26h11-raw-milk-cheese-september-2026
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/sierra-nevada-cheese-company-recalls-graziers-raw-milk-cheese-because-possible-health-risk

2) Bettergoods Authentic Italian Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine — Listeria (Walmart)
Gias Foods recalled 2 lots of frozen Bettergoods Authentic Italian Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine sold nationwide at Walmart: 22 oz yellow package, UPC 194346442706, lot L6079C (exp. 09/19/2027) or L6080C (exp. 09/20/2027). Potential Listeria monocytogenes. No illnesses reported. Do not eat; return to Walmart for refund or discard. Highest risk for pregnant people, young children, older adults, and anyone immunocompromised.
https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/MIDARD/bulletins/42c4adc
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/gias-foods-inc-recalls-bettergoods-authentic-italian-lemon-alfredo-fettuccine-because-possible

3) Cherry Oil / Old West End gasoline release — repairs still underway
Gasoline entered sewers after a vehicle hit a pump at Cherry St & Central Ave (Cherry Oil / Sunoco). Leak isolated from the sewer; underground repairs and air monitoring continued as of Sept 29; station remains closed; Ohio EPA and city Environmental Services overseeing. Owner hoped for a fix this week. If you smell gasoline indoors nearby: ventilate, wet cloths / fill P-traps, cover drains; report to Engage Toledo at 419-936-2020.
https://www.13abc.com/2026/09/29/repairs-underway-toledo-gas-station-after-gas-leak-driver-still-missing/
https://toledo.oh.gov/news/2026/09/22/city-investigating-gasoline-odor-reports

4) Berlin Seeds alfalfa sprouting seed — STEC / Salmonella (Ohio)
About 950 lbs Berlin Seeds Alfalfa Sprouting Seed, lot SAL2-24JB (5-lb/50-lb; smaller bags may lack lot). Sold Feb 13–Aug 23, 2026 from Millersburg, OH and online to 44 states including OH and MI. Stop using; discard seed and any sprouts; sanitize equipment.
https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/berlin-seeds-recalls-alfalfa-sprouting-seed-because-possible-health-risk

Invasive species
Spotted lanternfly is in egg-laying season (Oct–Dec). Statewide quarantine still covers all 88 Ohio counties — scrape gray waxy egg masses into soapy water or rubbing alcohol; check vehicles, firewood, outdoor gear. ODA no longer asks for routine sighting reports under the statewide quarantine. Box tree moth (Lorain Co.) and western Lake Erie grass carp control remain background items.
