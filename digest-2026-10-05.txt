EMERGING TECH WATCH
- Geoengineering
  - Ohio and Michigan bills (background, flagged first; no new action found in 48 hours)
    - Ohio HB 290, the "Atmosphere Protection Act," would ban solar radiation modification, cloud seeding and sunlight-reflection methods with felony penalties. Introduced May 20, 2025; still in the House Natural Resources Committee (last hearing Feb 18, 2026).
      - Source: https://ohiohouse.gov/legislation/136/hb290
    - Michigan HB 4304 would ban intentional releases into the air meant to change weather or temperature. Introduced Mar 26, 2025; sitting in the Regulatory Reform Committee with no further action.
      - Source: https://app.legiplex.com/mi/legislature/2025/2025-r/bills/hb4304
  - Drone cloud seeding near Homer, Alaska draws local pushback (republished Oct 4; original story Sep 21)
    - What happened: Rainmaker Technology Corp. (California) says it dispersed about 375 grams of silver iodide from a drone on Aug 23 near Kachemak Bay and produced about 19 million gallons of precipitation (roughly 0.01 inch over 100 square miles).
    - Reaction: the Kenai Peninsula Borough mayor plans an ordinance for local oversight; state Rep. Sarah Vance sent a cease-and-desist letter; Alaska DEC is reviewing. The company says it had FAA approval and notified state land managers; the state's natural resources agency says it has no authority over cloud seeding and issued no specific permit.
    - Confirmed vs. claimed: the operation and the official responses are confirmed; the rainfall amount is the company's own figure, and experts have long questioned how well seeding works.
    - Source: https://www.adn.com/alaska-journal/news/2026/10/04/a-california-cloud-seeding-company-says-it-created-rain-near-homer-it-also-created-an-uproar/
  - Malaysia steps up cloud seeding against haze (Oct 3)
    - What happened: 14 government cloud-seeding operations with more than 50 flights by Sep 30, using salt-based wet and dry seeding, to boost rain, ease smoke haze from Indonesian peat fires and keep reservoirs up. The Sepang circuit's CEO said seeding could be done around the track before this weekend's Grand Prix.
    - Source: https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/03/how-does-cloud-seeding-work-what-it-could-do-for-haze-at-sepang-this-weekend/237311
