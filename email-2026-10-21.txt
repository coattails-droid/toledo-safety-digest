NEEDS ATTENTION
- NEW: North Toledo chemical release - EVACUATION (1 mile) and SHELTER-IN-PLACE (3 miles) around MC Tank, 1266 Progress Ave.
  - What happened: a tank at the MC Tank Transport terminal began leaking around 10:48 p.m. Tue; release still ongoing per the city's 7:30 a.m. update. Early reports named Sterling Pipe & Tube / 1050 Progress Ave.; WTOL and the city now place it at MC Tank (the city page still centers the zones on 1050 Progress in places; the two addresses are close). The company says the chemical is glycidyl methacrylate (used to make plastics, paints and coatings). City: an irritant that can burn eyes, nose, mouth and skin; overexposure can cause headache, dizziness, tiredness, nausea, vomiting. No injuries, no explosion. EPA is monitoring air with Toledo Fire.
  - Within 1 mile: evacuate. Shelter at Shoreland Elementary; Red Cross and TARTA buses at the Meijer lot off E. Alexis Rd. Includes Raintree Village mobile home park and the Alexis Addition neighborhood; workers report Toledo Assembly Complex was evacuated (not confirmed by Stellantis).
  - Within 3 miles (issued 4:24 a.m.): stay indoors, close windows and doors, turn off ventilation/furnace fans that pull in outside air, keep pets inside.
  - Outside the zones and smell something? Do the same. Dense fog makes haze look the same as the plume, so don't judge by sight.
  - Feeling unwell: mild eye/skin/throat irritation, shower or rinse and call a doctor; trouble breathing, vomiting, dizziness or confusion, call 911 or go to the ER. Health questions: Toledo-Lucas County Health Dept 419-213-4100. Non-emergency questions: Engage Toledo 419-936-2020 (the city says it only has what's been released); don't tie up 911. Updates: City of Toledo site, Toledo Fire social media, Lucas County EMA alerts.
  - Closures: Toledo Public Schools and Washington Local Schools closed today; Enterprise Blvd (Stickney to Benore) and Matzinger Rd closed; nearby rail lines shut. Expect detours around I-75/Alexis.
  - Sources: https://toledo.oh.gov/news/2026/10/21/hazmat-evacuation-in-north-toledo and https://www.13abc.com/2026/10/21/hazmat-crews-responding-hazardous-materials-leak-north-toledo/ and https://www.wtol.com/article/news/local/north-toledo-hazmat-situation-evacuations/512-23d84bbb-60fc-4c49-aaf4-57ca35a7f8c6
