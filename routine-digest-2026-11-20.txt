NEEDS ATTENTION
- MC Tank (1266 Progress Ave) chemical release and Brent Industries explosion: council hearing held Nov 19 (updated)
  - Toledo Fire & Rescue Deputy Chief Michael Posadny said fog grounded drones and made the plume hard to track ("it kind of worked like a top"), which is why the 3-mile shelter-in-place was ordered. Response involved 15 agencies and 50+ firefighters.
  - City incident timeline (as reported): 911 call 9:30 p.m. Oct 20; leave-within-1-mile order 10:48 p.m.; 3-mile shelter-in-place 4:24 a.m. Residents said alerts came late (one phone alert at 1:57 a.m.) or never, and sirens did not sound.
  - Health Commissioner Karim Baroudi: chemical known to be a carcinogen; no immediate public danger, long-term effects uncertain; department continues monitoring. Councilwoman Gadus said the affected area may be wider than the shelter zone.
  - MC Tank and Brent representatives did not attend. Committee chair Nick Komives: city will post common questions and answers online; another public meeting planned for December.
  - Source: https://www.13abc.com/2026/11/20/toledo-council-hears-first-responders-response-two-industrial-incidents/ ; https://www.wtol.com/article/news/health/neighbors-demand-accountability-at-toledo-city-council-committee-hearing-after-toledo-industrial-incidents-ohio/512-8336b56e-e6fa-408e-a2ea-112744d9cc1a ; https://lakeerieinquiry.org/stories/2026-11-20-2
- Toyota/Lexus Takata front-passenger airbag inflator recall (ongoing; NHTSA 26V748000; 314,029 vehicles)
  - Models: 2002-2010 Lexus SC430, 2011 IS250, 2017 GX460, 2003-2008 Corolla, 2005-2006 Tundra, 2005-2007 Sequoia, 2016-2017 4Runner. Inflator may rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin: injury or death risk.
  - Remedy: dealer replaces inflator or airbag assembly free; owner letters start Dec 28, 2026. Toyota numbers 26TB25, 26TA25, 26LB11, 26LA11; Toyota 1-800-331-4331. Check VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or toyota.com/recall.
  - Source: https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-recalls-front-passenger-airbags-in-certain-vehicles/ ; https://recallbench.com/recall/nhtsa-26v748000/
- Magnetic robot toys (new; CPSC Nov 19; CHE-US; ~101,282 units)
  - Arms/legs release high-powered magnets; ingestion can cause intestinal perforation, blockage, death. Stop use, take away from children, contact CHE-US for refund (photo of toys in trash to CHE-USMagneticRobotToysRecall@outlook.com).
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/CHE-US-Recalls-Magnetic-Robot-Toys-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Magnet-Ingestion-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Toys
- Hultafors Group lighted portfolios, angle locators, calipers, calculators (new; CPSC Nov 19; ~229,430 units)
  - Brands CLC and Johnson Level. Button batteries accessible to children; swallowing can cause fatal burns. Keep away from children; refund via www.HG2026.expertinquiry.com (photo and disposal certification).
  - Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Hultafors-Group-North-America-Recalls-Lighted-Portfolios-Angle-Locators-Calipers-and-Calculators-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Battery-Ingestion-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Consumer-Products-with-Button-Cell-Batteries
- Xentrawave adult portable bed rails, model XT016 (new; CPSC; ~2,114 units; one death)
  - Entrapment/asphyxiation; violates federal standard. Stop using. Details at cpsc.gov.
- Sharper Image fire pits (ongoing; CPSC; ~265,000) and SHEIN baby rattle/teether sets (ongoing; ~2,657): see checklist; fire pits can jet burning fuel; sets can shed choking parts.
